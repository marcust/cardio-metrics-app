Cardio Metrics: Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Who uses the app

Cardio Metrics is a personal Google Apps Script project. Its only user is its owner, Marcus More-Thiesen. It is not offered to or used by anyone else.

What data it accesses

With the owner's consent, the app reads the following from the owner's Google Health data through the Google Health API, read-only: daily cardio fitness (VO2max), daily resting heart rate, and heart rate aggregated per minute. It also reads and writes the Google Sheet it is bound to.

How the data is used and stored

The values are written into the owner's own private Google Sheet, where they are used to follow personal training and health trends. The app keeps no other copy and runs no servers of its own; everything stays inside the owner's Google account.

Sharing

The data is not shared with, sold or transferred to anyone. It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to train machine-learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Cardio Metrics' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The data written so far can be deleted by deleting the rows in the sheet.
